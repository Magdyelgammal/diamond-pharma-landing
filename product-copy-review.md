## Editorial decisions

- D3+K2 Mond is displayed as 60 soft gelatin capsules, matching the front photograph. The separately supplied facts image specifies 30 servings at one capsule each; this conflicts with the front count and is recorded for owner confirmation. The composition is shown per capsule without copying the disputed container-servings value. “K2-M7” is transcribed as supplied rather than silently changed to “MK-7”. Daily-value percentages are omitted.
- The D3+K2 inputs do not contain a readable dosing schedule. The website does not infer a daily dose from the one-capsule serving size. The general medication caution is based on NIH ingredient guidance rather than a claimed transcription of an unseen warning panel.

- Royal Mond now uses the owner's latest 30-capsule `royal 2.png` image. Its side panel appears different from the earlier 10-count label and is not sufficiently legible to reliably transcribe all amounts. The old 333.33 mg per-softgel figure and one-capsule daily instruction have been replaced with the front-of-pack 1000 mg declaration and a reference to the matching label. Exact formulation, serving basis and dose require confirmation from the 30-count facts panel. Energy/immunity claims have not been extrapolated from royal jelly.
- Sawmond has no claim to treat prostate enlargement, urinary symptoms or hair loss. The proposed benefit concerns nutritional zinc intake.
- Melato Mond has no claim to treat insomnia or a sleep disorder. Adult-only directions and the packaging's driving/alcohol cautions are retained.
- The owner supplied a real 60-gummy Melato Mond bottle to replace the earlier 15-count presentation. Both the website pack-size text and the image now show 60 gummies. The real purple bottle and purple cap replace the prior presentation assumptions. The 60-count rendered artwork has the same per-gummy composition, so the ingredient amounts are unchanged.
- All seven Lozin labels list the same three active amounts. Each flavor has its own other-ingredients disclosure. Sugar-free lemon lists maltitol, isomalt, xylitol and stevia, while the other variants list sucrose/glucose syrup. Ginger and honey are labelled flavors; they are not presented as additional active ingredients.
- The sugar-free Lozin artwork says 24 on the front but 16 servings in the facts panel. The website keeps the 24-count pack explicitly requested by the owner; the source facts-panel count requires correction/confirmation before using a final printable label.
- The available VitaMond PDF describes a 30-gummy version, while the approved image shows 120 gummies. The nutrient names agree with the supplied image. Exact nutrient amounts and dosage are therefore not copied from the 30-count PDF to the 120-count product.
- Percentage daily values are omitted; they are not needed for the landing page and some label percentages require a separate nutrition-panel review.
- This is a company/product landing page. The label excerpts do not replace the full directions, contraindications or professional advice.

## General ingredient reference checks

These describe ingredient roles or evidence limitations; they are not clinical trials of the finished Diamond products.

- [NIH ODS: Vitamin C](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminC-Consumer/): normal immune function and antioxidant activity.
- [NIH ODS: Immune Function](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Immunefunction-Consumer/): adequate nutrient intake supports immune function; extra supplementation does not generally prevent infection in people without deficiency.
- [NCCIH: Melatonin](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/melatonin-what-you-need-to-know): evidence and safety limitations; no insomnia-treatment promise is made here.
- [NCCIH: Saw Palmetto](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/saw-palmetto): no prostate/urinary treatment claim is made here.
- [NIH ODS: Vitamin D](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminD-Consumer/): calcium absorption, bone health, normal muscle and immune function; strength is not a recommendation for unsupervised daily use.
- [NIH ODS: Vitamin K](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminK-Consumer/): interaction with warfarin.
